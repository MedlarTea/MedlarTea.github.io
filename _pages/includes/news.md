- *2026.07*: Check out our latest work, "ReferTrack: Referring Then Tracking for Embodied Visual Tracking." This work introduces a *referring-then-tracking* paradigm that first grounds a language-described target to an image-space bounding box and then decodes tracking waypoints from this decision, using TVBI tokens to preserve target motion cues over time. It achieves state-of-the-art single-view performance on EVT-Bench with robust sim-to-real transfer to legged and humanoid robots. Learn more on [project page](https://medlartea.github.io/referTrack/).
