I am currently a Senior Researcher at [Tencent Robotics X Lab](https://roboticsx.tencent.com/). I received my Ph.D. in the [Robotics and Computer Vision Lab](https://rcvlab.eee.sustech.edu.cn/) at Southern University of Science and Technology, under the guidance of [Prof. Hong ZHANG](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=J7UkpAIAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate) in 2026.
